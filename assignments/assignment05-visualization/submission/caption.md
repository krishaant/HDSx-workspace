# Figure Caption

Mean BMI by household income group across all 11 NHANES cycles (1999-2000
to 2021-2023), adults aged 20-80 (n = 50,851).

- **Statistic:** mean BMI per cycle × income group (PIR-based: Low <1.3,
  Middle, High >3.5).
- **Source:** classroom NHANES snapshot,
  `examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv` (CDC/NCHS public-use
  data, prepared for classroom use).
- **Exclusions:** restricted to ages 20-80; excludes 6,286 adult records
  missing `BMI` or `IncomeGroup`.
- **Limitations:** descriptive and unweighted — NHANES sample weights were
  not applied, so these are sample statistics for this classroom file, not
  population-representative estimates, and this cross-sectional comparison
  does not show that income causes differences in BMI.
